Frequently Asked Questions

How do I clone my custom voice model?

Go to Voice Library, click Cloned Voices, and upload at least 5 minutes of high-quality, noise-free voice clips. The system generates your cloning settings automatically.

What is the API key requests quota limit?

Free plans have a limit of 1,000 monthly requests. Pro tiers allow up to 50,000 monthly requests. Enterprise options are fully customizable.

Can I refund invoice payments?

Yes, you can request invoice refunds within 14 days of purchase. Head to Billing and click Request Refund next to the invoice item.
